CryptoSafe is a lightweight, offline-first Android app for military-grade text encryption with Anonymous Encrypted Boxes. Your data never leaves your device.

Features:
• Strong Encryption: AES-256-GCM with Argon2id key derivation (128 MiB, 4 iterations)
• Encrypted Boxes: Organize conversations in password-protected boxes with per-box passwords
• App Lock: PIN and Biometric (fingerprint) unlock with auto-lock timer
• Encrypted Database: All data stored in SQLCipher encrypted database
• 100% Offline: No internet permission needed
• Share to Box: Receive text shared from WhatsApp, Telegram, and other apps
• Auto-delete Messages: Set messages to auto-delete after 1 hour to 90 days
• Screenshot Protection: FLAG_SECURE prevents accidental leaks
• Password Generator: Built-in random password generator
• Modern UI: Material 3 design with dark theme
